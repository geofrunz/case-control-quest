Debrief · Think critically

What might mislead our investigation?

Recall bias

Cases may remember what they ate differently from controls.

Selection bias

Controls must represent the exposure distribution in the population that produced the cases.

Confounding

Mayonnaise consumption might be associated with another contaminated food.

Etiological inference

An elevated OR is a clue; food testing, clinical microbiology and outbreak investigation provide additional evidence.

Instructor note: The festival and all numerical data are fictional. This is a teaching simulation, not an actual outbreak. Participants can interact without accounts; scores are not collected or sent to the instructor.
